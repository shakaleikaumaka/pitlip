# PIT LIP 💋 — Leave It Prettier

The 9th fork of the [P.I.T. Protocol](https://publicinform.com) — a podcast of brotherly love, public goods, and prettier worlds.

**LIP = Leave It Prettier** — an acronym Timour (co-founder of Edge City) gave the village at Edge Esmeralda (Healdsburg, June 2026). It spread mouth to mouth, town hall to town hall, until it rivaled "leave no trace" itself — because it isn't just about not harming. It's about *adding beauty wherever you stand*.

This pad holds the verbatim LIP quotes pulled straight from the [Esmeralda P.I.T.](https://esmeraldapit.com) transcripts, and the declared **Shaka × Timour** monthly podcast (status: *Proposed · Invitation Delivered*) — every episode to ride the P.I.T. stack: recorded in the open, transcribed within a day, published consent-first, archived forever.
